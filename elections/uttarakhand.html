FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

Do you deliver election umbrellas to small towns in Uttarakhand?

Yes. We dispatch from our Bhiwandi factory by transport or courier to any town or district headquarters in Uttarakhand. Delivery is charged extra, depending on the town and order size.

Can you print the party symbol, colours and candidate name?

Yes. We print party colours, the election symbol, the candidate's photo and name, or a slogan, with a PDF mock-up for approval before printing. Screen printing suits up to 3 colours; digital printing handles photos and any number of colours.

What is the minimum order?

Printed umbrellas: 300 pieces. Printed garden umbrellas: 12 pieces. Canopy tents and gazebos: from 1 piece.

Do you give a GST invoice for the election expense account?

Yes. Every order comes with a GST invoice (GSTIN 27AGCPJ9001C1ZX), which candidates can use for their election expenditure records.

When should we order?

As early as possible. Printing capacity fills up when many candidates order in the same weeks before polling, so send your requirement as soon as your campaign dates and quantities are clear.

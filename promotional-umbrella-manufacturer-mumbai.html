Mukund Enterprises has made promotional umbrellas for over a decade. Our founder, Mr. Pankaj Jha, has around 35 years in the umbrella industry. Every umbrella is made under one roof at our Bhiwandi factory, so we control the fabric, the stitching and the printing.

We print your logo by screen printing (up to 3 colours) or digital printing (any colour, matched to your hex code), on up to 8 panels. You see a PDF mock-up before anything is printed.

How ordering works

From logo to delivery

1. Share your logo

WhatsApp or email your logo (CDR or PDF), the product and the quantity.

2. Approve the mock-up

We send a PDF mock-up showing exactly how it will look. Printing starts only after you approve.

3. Production

Made at our own factory in Bhiwandi, with quality and packing checks on every order.

4. Delivery

Delivered across India with a GST invoice, or collected from Dombivli West or Bhiwandi by appointment.

Questions

Frequently asked

Are you a manufacturer or a trader?

We are a manufacturer. All our umbrellas are made at our own factory in Bhiwandi, except a few speciality styles such as capsule and reverse umbrellas, which are imported.

What is the minimum order for logo printed umbrellas?

300 pieces. Plain umbrellas without printing start at 144 pieces (12 dozen).

Which printing do you use?

Screen printing for logos with up to 3 colours, and digital printing for logos with more colours. With digital printing on white fabric we can match any brand colour from its hex code.

How long does a promotional umbrella order take?

Production time depends on quantity and printing. TO CONFIRM: usual lead time WhatsApp us with your date and we will confirm before you order.

Can I visit the factory?

Yes, by appointment. After Diwali through to August the Bhiwandi factory runs 24/7 and you can see samples there.

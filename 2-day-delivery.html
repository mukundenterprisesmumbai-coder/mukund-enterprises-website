Why we can

Our own factory

Most canopy and umbrella sellers buy the frame from one supplier and send the fabric to another printer, which takes a week or more. We do screen and digital printing, stitching and framing in our own factory in Bhiwandi, near Mumbai, which runs 24/7 from Diwali through August.

  • Over a decade in business; founder with about 35 years in umbrellas
  • Screen printing for 1 to 3 colour logos, digital printing for any colour
  • No rush fee: delivery is charged by city and order size

2-day delivery applies to standard sizes and regular quantities. For large bulk orders, such as thousands of logo umbrellas, we confirm the date in the quote.

FAQ

2-day delivery questions

Can you really print and deliver a canopy in 2 days?

Yes. Printing, stitching and framing all happen in our own factory in Bhiwandi, which runs 24/7 from Diwali through August. For standard sizes and regular quantities, your order reaches Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Pune, Nashik or South Gujarat within 2 days of mock-up approval and advance payment.

When does the 2 days start?

From the time you approve the PDF mock-up and pay the 50% advance. Send your logo (CDR or PDF) with the enquiry so the mock-up is ready quickly.

Which products can be delivered in 2 days?

Printed 10x10 pop-up canopies, gazebo tents, canopy tents with side walls, big stall umbrellas, printed garden umbrellas and promotional tables. Logo-printed umbrellas in bulk (300+ pieces) depend on quantity; we confirm the date in the quote.

Is there an extra charge for 2-day delivery?

No rush fee. Delivery is charged by city and order size and added to your quote.

Which areas get 2-day delivery?

Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Kalyan-Dombivli, Bhiwandi, Vasai-Virar and Palghar, Panvel and Raigad, Pune, Nashik, Lonavala and Alibaug, and Surat, Vapi and South Gujarat. We deliver to the rest of India by transport or courier.

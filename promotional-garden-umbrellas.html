Who uses branded garden umbrellas

Beverage, ice-cream, dairy and snack brands give them to their retailers and vendors; telecom, fintech and insurance companies use them at kiosks and sales points; cafés, resorts and clubs use them over outdoor seating; and dealers use them to make their shop visible from the road.

Because the minimum order is only 12 pieces, a single dealer, a new café or a small pilot campaign can order printed umbrellas – not just large brands.

Choosing the size

6 ft – single table, small kiosk or vendor cart.

7 ft – the popular all-rounder for shop fronts and outdoor seating.

8 ft – largest garden size, for bigger tables, stalls and maximum logo visibility.

For larger spaces we also make 10 ft centre-pole umbrellas and side-pole (cantilever) umbrellas.

Printing on all panels

We print by screen printing (best for logos with up to 3 colours) and digital printing (any number of colours, with exact colour matching from your hex code). Send your logo as CDR or PDF – we share a PDF mock-up and start printing only after you approve it.

Every panel can carry your logo, so the brand is visible from every direction.

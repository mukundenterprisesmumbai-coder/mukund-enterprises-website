Our story


Mukund Enterprises is a reliable supplier of high-quality umbrellas, gazebos and canopies at affordable prices. We manufacture umbrellas and also deal in gazebos, canopies, promotional tables and more, from our two locations in Kalbadevi, Mumbai and Dombivli West. The business is owned and managed by Mr. Pankaj Jha.

We direct all our activities towards meeting customer expectations with excellent quality products. We follow ethical business policies and complete transparency in every transaction, so that the relationships we build with customers stay healthy and long-lasting.

Our range covers folding and promotional umbrellas, beach and garden umbrellas, canopy tents, display tents and gazebos, all available with custom branding.

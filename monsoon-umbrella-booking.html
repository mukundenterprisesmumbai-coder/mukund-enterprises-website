Monsoon 2027 · book early

Book Your Monsoon 2027 Umbrellas Early

Companies hand out branded umbrellas just before the rains, and every maker gets busy from April. Booking between November and March gets you the best rate, your choice of colours and a print slot that is ready before June.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

Why book before March

  • Better rates: our rates are lowest in November and rise gradually until March.
  • Your colours: fabric in your brand colour is easier to arrange with time in hand.
  • On time: umbrellas reach your offices and dealers before the rains start in June, not after.
  • Time to approve: you get a PDF mock-up and can review it with your team without a rush.

A simple monsoon plan

  • November to January: decide the quantity and style, share your logo, approve the mock-up.
  • February to March: confirm the order with the advance; production and printing at our factory.
  • April to May: dispatch to your offices, branches or dealers across India.
  • June: the rains arrive and your brand is already out on the street.

Popular monsoon gifts

3-fold auto-open umbrellas with a silver coating fit a laptop bag and are the most popular staff gift. 2-fold auto-open umbrellas give a larger canopy, and long straight J-handle umbrellas give the biggest logo area for dealers and field teams. How ordering works

From your logo to delivery

Step 1

Share your requirement

WhatsApp +91 91378 78456 or send the enquiry form with the product, quantity, city and your logo. We usually reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes.

Step 2

Get a quote and mock-up

We confirm the price and send a PDF mock-up showing exactly where and how your logo will be printed.

Step 3

Approve and pay the advance

Production starts after you approve the mock-up. Customised orders: 50% advance, balance before dispatch. GST invoice with every order.

Step 4

Production at our Bhiwandi factory

Your order is made and printed under one roof at our own factory in Bhiwandi and checked before packing.

Step 5

Delivery or collection

We deliver across India, or you can collect from our Dombivli West shop or Bhiwandi factory outlet (by appointment).

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

When should we order umbrellas for monsoon gifting?

Between November and March. Rates are lowest in November and rise gradually until March, and an early order is ready before the rains in June.

What is the minimum order for logo-printed umbrellas?

300 pieces.

Can we see how our logo will look first?

Yes. We send a PDF mock-up showing exactly where and how your logo will be printed before production starts.

Can you deliver to all our branches?

Yes. We dispatch across India by transport or courier. Delivery is charged extra.

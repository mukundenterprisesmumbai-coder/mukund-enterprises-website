How ordering works

From your logo to delivery

Step 1

Share your requirement

WhatsApp +91 91378 78456 or send the enquiry form with the product, quantity, city and your logo. We usually reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes.

Step 2

Get a quote and mock-up

We confirm the price and send a PDF mock-up showing exactly where and how your logo will be printed.

Step 3

Approve and pay the advance

Production starts after you approve the mock-up. Customised orders: 50% advance, balance before dispatch. GST invoice with every order.

Step 4

Production at our Bhiwandi factory

Your order is made and printed under one roof at our own factory in Bhiwandi and checked before packing.

Step 5

Delivery or collection

We deliver across India, or you can collect from our Dombivli West shop or Bhiwandi factory outlet (by appointment).

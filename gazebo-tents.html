Why a pop-up gazebo

A folding (pop-up) gazebo is one of the quickest ways to set up a branded space: the frame folds out, the printed top goes on, and it folds back down for the next location. That is why event managers, promoters and sales teams like it for roadshows that move from place to place.

Plain gazebos are useful for shade and seating at weddings, functions, societies and shops; printed gazebos double as a billboard.

Sizes and uses

  • 6x6 ft – promoter desk, kiosk, small stall.
  • 10x10 ft – the most popular size for events and exhibitions.
  • 10x15 / 10x20 ft – product demos, seating and large display areas.

Branding

We print by screen printing (best for logos with up to 3 colours) and digital printing (any number of colours, with exact colour matching from your hex code). Send your logo as CDR or PDF – we share a PDF mock-up and start printing only after you approve it.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

What is the difference between a gazebo and a canopy tent?

They are very similar – both are open-sided tents for shade and branding. 'Pop-up gazebo' usually means a tent with a folding frame that sets up quickly.

Can I order one gazebo?

Yes, from 1 piece.

Which sizes are available?

6x6, 10x10, 10x15 and 10x20 ft, and other standard sizes.

Can you print our logo on the gazebo?

Yes – screen, digital and other printing options, with a PDF mock-up first.

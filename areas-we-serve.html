Who buys from us

Industry

Banks, NBFCs & Insurance

Mumbai is home to the head offices of India's banks, NBFCs and insurers – and their branding reaches every branch, loan camp and field agent across the country. We make it.

Industry

FMCG, Beverage & Retail Brands

Turn every retailer, vendor cart and outlet into a branded spot. Garden umbrellas from just 12 pieces, and canopies for sampling and activations.

Industry

Event, BTL & Branding Agencies

Branding and event agencies are among our regular customers. Buy direct from the factory, get mock-ups your client can approve, and deliver activations on time.

Industry

Real Estate Developers

New projects across Thane, Navi Mumbai, Panvel and the Mumbai suburbs need visible branding at the site, at launch events and at weekend expos. We supply it.

Industry

Election & Campaign Material

Political parties are among our regular customers. We print party colours, symbols and candidate branding on umbrellas, canopies and gazebos – in bulk, on schedule.

Industry

Hotels, Resorts & Cafés

Garden, centre-pole and side-pole umbrellas and gazebos for poolsides, lawns, terraces and outdoor seating – plain, or printed with your logo or a beverage partner's brand.

Industry

Agri-input & Rural Marketing

Umbrellas are one of the most useful gifts for farmers and rural dealers – and canopies are the stage for every farmer meet. We supply both in bulk.

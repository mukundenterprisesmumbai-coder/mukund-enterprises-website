Garden umbrella size guide: 6, 7 or 8 ft?

Big branded umbrellas make a shop front, café or stall visible from across the road. Here is how to pick the right size and what it costs, with printed orders from just 12 pieces.

Which size do you need?

  • 6 ft covers a single table, a small kiosk or a vendor cart.
  • 7 ft is the popular all-rounder for shop fronts and outdoor seating.
  • 8 ft is the largest garden size, for bigger tables, stalls and the most logo visibility.

For bigger spaces we also make 10 ft centre-pole umbrellas and side-pole (cantilever) umbrellas, which keep the pole out of the way of the table.

Stand or no stand?

Order the stand if the umbrella will stand on its own on a pavement, terrace or lawn. Skip it if the umbrella goes through a table that already has a hole, or if your outlets already have bases from an earlier campaign.

Tell us where the umbrellas will be used when you enquire and we will suggest the right stand.

Printing on every panel

We print on all panels, so your brand is visible from every direction. Screen printing suits logos with up to 3 colours. Digital printing handles any number of colours and can match your exact brand colour from its hex code.

Send your logo as CDR or PDF. We share a PDF mock-up and start printing only after you approve it.

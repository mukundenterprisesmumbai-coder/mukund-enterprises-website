The six things that set the price

  • Type and size. A 21" 2-fold umbrella uses less fabric and a lighter frame than a 27" 3-fold or a long J-handle umbrella, so it costs less.
  • Fabric and finish. Satin, piping, silver (UV) coating and auto-open add to the cost but make the umbrella feel premium.
  • Printing method. Screen printing suits logos with up to 3 colours. Logos with more colours are printed digitally. The more colours, the higher the printing cost.
  • Number of prints. An umbrella has 8 panels, so you can print 1 to 8 times. Each extra print adds a little.
  • Quantity. Logo-printed orders start at 300 pieces and plain orders at 144 pieces (12 dozen). Bigger orders get a better rate.
  • When you book. Rates are lowest in November and rise month by month until March.

How to get the best rate

  • Book between November and March, and as early as you can, for a monsoon campaign.
  • If your logo works in 1 to 3 colours, screen printing keeps the cost down.
  • Send the logo as CDR or PDF so we can quote and make the mock-up straight away.
  • Combine departments or branches into one order to reach a bigger quantity slab.
  • Buy from the manufacturer. We make and print everything in our own factory in Bhiwandi, so there is no middleman margin.

Which umbrella for which budget

Lowest cost per piece: 2-fold umbrellas, for mass giveaways, dealer schemes and campaigns.

Mid range: 27" 3-fold and long J-handle umbrellas, with a bigger canopy and more room for the logo.

Premium gifts: 3-fold auto-open with silver coating, often with custom packaging. See corporate gift umbrellas.

Outdoor branding: garden umbrellas for shops and stalls. See our garden umbrella size guide.

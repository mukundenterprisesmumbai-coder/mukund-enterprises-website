Canopy tent vs gazebo: which one to buy

Both give you a branded, shaded space for events, exhibitions and sales counters. Here is how they differ, which size to pick and what they cost. You can order from a single piece.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

The short answer

They are very similar. Both are open-sided tents for shade and branding. "Pop-up gazebo" usually means a tent with a folding frame that sets up in minutes and folds back down for the next location. That makes it the usual choice for roadshows and activations that move from place to place.

A promotional canopy tent or printed display tent is built around your branding, for a stall or sales point that people should notice from a distance.

Which size?

  • 6x6 ft for a promoter desk, kiosk or small stall.
  • 10x10 ft, the standard event and exhibition size. See the 10x10 pop-up canopy.
  • 10x15 and 10x20 ft for product demos, seating and large display areas.

What can be printed

Tell us which faces you want printed: the roof panels, the valance (the hanging flap) or the side walls. We show all of it on a PDF mock-up before printing.

Screen printing suits logos with up to 3 colours. Digital printing handles any number of colours, with exact colour matching from your hex code.

Side walls and add-ons

Add side walls for sun, wind and rain, or to turn the canopy into a closed stall. A printed promotional table in front completes the setup for sampling and sales camps.

One piece or hundreds

Canopies and gazebos can be ordered from a single piece for your own stall. Agencies and brands running state-wide activations order in bulk at wholesale rates. Everything is made and printed in our own factory in Bhiwandi.

Reverse (Inverted) Umbrella

An inverted umbrella that folds inside-out, so the wet side stays inside when you close it. Easy to close when getting into a car or doorway, and a popular modern gift.

Manufactured and supplied by Mukund Enterprises, with offices in Kalbadevi (Mumbai) and Dombivli West and our own factory in Bhiwandi. We supply single samples, bulk orders and custom-branded pieces to dealers, retailers and companies across Mumbai and Maharashtra.

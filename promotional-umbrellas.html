Why brands choose promotional umbrellas

An umbrella is one of the few giveaways people actually use – every day of the monsoon, in public, with your logo open over their head. That makes it one of the most visible branding items for its cost, which is why banks, insurance companies, FMCG brands, real-estate developers and political campaigns order them in bulk every season.

We manufacture under one roof at our own factory in Bhiwandi, so you deal directly with the maker – no middleman between your purchase team and the printing table.

Printing options

We print by screen printing (best for logos with up to 3 colours) and digital printing (any number of colours, with exact colour matching from your hex code). Send your logo as CDR or PDF – we share a PDF mock-up and start printing only after you approve it.

Standard Indian umbrellas have 8 panels, so your logo can be printed up to 8 times – once on each panel – or you can mix your logo, tagline and website across panels.

Which umbrella suits your campaign?

  • Folding umbrellas (2-fold and 3-fold) – compact gifts for employees, customers and dealers.
  • Long straight umbrellas (J-handle) – a large canopy with maximum logo area for field staff and events.
  • Auto-open umbrellas – a premium feel for corporate gifting.
  • Garden and centre-pole umbrellas – outdoor branding for shops, outlets and stalls. See promotional garden umbrellas.

Book early for the best rate

Prices are lowest in November and rise gradually until March. If your monsoon campaign is fixed, booking between November and March gets you the best price and production slot.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page and in our promotional umbrella price guide.

What is the minimum order for promotional umbrellas?

300 pieces for logo-printed umbrellas. Plain umbrellas start at 144 pieces (12 dozen).

How many times can you print my logo?

Up to 8 times – one print on each of the 8 panels.

Can you match our brand colour exactly?

Yes. With digital printing on white fabric we can reproduce any colour from your hex code.

Do you give samples?

Yes, for orders of 5,000 pieces or more. A printed sample costs ₹500.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Yes, we deliver across India.

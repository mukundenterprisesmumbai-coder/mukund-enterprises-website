Made for brands on the move

A printed canopy turns any open space – a mall atrium, a society gate, a petrol pump, a highway dhaba or an exhibition aisle – into your own branded booth in minutes. Event and BTL agencies, telecom and fintech companies, banks, FMCG brands and real-estate developers use them for activations, loan and SIM camps, product sampling and site events.

You can order a single canopy for your own stall or hundreds for a state-wide activation; bulk orders get wholesale pricing.

Sizes

6x6 ft – compact kiosk or promoter desk.

10x10 ft – the standard event and exhibition canopy.

10x15 ft and 10x20 ft – bigger stalls, demos and seating areas.

Need side walls? See canopy tent with side walls.

Printing your canopy

We print by screen printing (best for logos with up to 3 colours) and digital printing (any number of colours, with exact colour matching from your hex code). Send your logo as CDR or PDF – we share a PDF mock-up and start printing only after you approve it.

Tell us which faces you want printed – roof panels, valance (the hanging flap) or side walls – and we will show it on the mock-up.

How ordering works

From your logo to delivery

Step 1

Share your requirement

WhatsApp +91 91378 78456 or send the enquiry form with the product, quantity, city and your logo. We usually reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes.

Step 2

Get a quote and mock-up

We confirm the price and send a PDF mock-up showing exactly where and how your logo will be printed.

Step 3

Approve and pay the advance

Production starts after you approve the mock-up. Customised orders: 50% advance, balance before dispatch. GST invoice with every order.

Your order is made and printed under one roof at our own factory in Bhiwandi and checked before packing.

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page and in our canopy tent vs gazebo guide.

Can I order a single printed canopy?

Yes, canopies are available from 1 piece. Bulk orders get wholesale pricing.

Which canopy sizes are available?

6x6, 10x10, 10x15 and 10x20 ft, and other standard market sizes.

Which printing do you offer?

Screen printing, digital printing and other printing solutions – we suggest the best one for your design.

Will I see the design before printing?

Yes, we send a PDF mock-up and print only after your approval.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai, across India?

Yes. We dispatch to every state from our Bhiwandi factory by transport or courier, including Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and Ahmedabad. Delivery is charged extra, depending on the city and order size.

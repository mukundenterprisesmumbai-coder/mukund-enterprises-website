FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

Which parties do you print umbrellas for?

Any registered party, independent candidates and their campaign agencies. Regular orders come from BJP, Congress, AAP, Samajwadi Party, BSP, Shiv Sena, NCP, MNS, TMC, DMK, AIADMK and Akali Dal units.

Who can order party-printed umbrellas?

Party units, candidates and their campaign agencies. Please send the artwork approved by your party office; we print it for you but don't supply party artwork or sell symbol-printed stock to the public.

What is the minimum order?

Printed umbrellas: 300 pieces. Printed garden umbrellas: 12 pieces. Canopy tents and gazebos: from 1 piece.

How long does printing take?

Within 2 days of mock-up approval for standard sizes in the Mumbai region (see 2-day printed delivery). For other states, add transport time; the date is confirmed in the quote. Book early before polling, when many candidates order in the same weeks.

Can the umbrellas be given out to voters?

Gifts to voters are not allowed while the model code of conduct is in force. During an election, use them for party workers, booths and rallies.

Do you give a GST invoice?

Yes, with every order (GSTIN 27AGCPJ9001C1ZX), for the candidate's election expenditure records.

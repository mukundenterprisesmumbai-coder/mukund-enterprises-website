Questions

Frequently asked

What is the difference between a gazebo and a canopy tent?

They are very similar. A gazebo usually means a folding, frame-supported tent with a peaked roof, often 10x10 ft, while canopy is the wider term for any shade tent. We make and print both.

Can you print a 10x10 gazebo with our logo?

Yes. Share your logo (CDR or PDF) and we send a mock-up for approval before printing.

Yes. Gazebos can be ordered from a single piece; the single-piece price differs from the wholesale price.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. You can also collect from our Dombivli West shop or Bhiwandi factory outlet by appointment.

How ordering works

From logo to delivery

1. Share your logo

WhatsApp or email your logo (CDR or PDF), the product and the quantity.

2. Approve the mock-up

We send a PDF mock-up showing exactly how it will look. Printing starts only after you approve.

3. Production

Made at our own factory in Bhiwandi, with quality and packing checks on every order.

4. Delivery

Delivered across India with a GST invoice, or collected from Dombivli West or Bhiwandi by appointment.

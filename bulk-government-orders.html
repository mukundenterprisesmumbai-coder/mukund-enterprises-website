Bulk & institutional buying

Umbrellas & Canopy Tents for Bulk and Government Orders

Government departments, municipal corporations, election offices, PSUs, banks, schools and NGOs buy umbrellas, canopy tents and gazebos from us in bulk. You get a formal quotation for your file, a GST invoice and delivery to any district in India.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

Paperwork your purchase file needs

  • Formal quotation on our letterhead with product specifications, photos, unit price, GST and delivery terms.
  • GST invoice with every order (GSTIN 27AGCPJ9001C1ZX).
  • Purchase orders accepted from departments, PSUs, banks and companies.
  • Product details for tenders: sizes, fabric, frame and print options, so you can write or check a specification.

Who buys in bulk from us

  • Municipal corporations and councils: canopy tents and umbrellas for ward offices, camps, drives and public events.
  • Election offices and polling teams: canopy tents and gazebos for shade at booths and counting centres, and umbrellas for field staff.
  • Traffic police and field staff: large umbrellas and sun umbrellas for duty points.
  • PSUs, banks and insurers: logo umbrellas for staff and customers, canopies for branch camps and loan melas.
  • Schools, colleges and NGOs: umbrellas for students, canopies for health camps and events.

Why buy from the factory

Every order is made and printed in our own factory in Bhiwandi, near Mumbai, so you deal directly with the maker and not a middleman. We have handled single orders of 2,000 to 5,000 dozen umbrellas and dispatch to every state by transport or courier.

Logo, department name or a public message (for example a safety or awareness slogan) can be printed on umbrellas, canopy tops and gazebo walls.

How ordering works

From your logo to delivery

Step 1

Share your requirement

WhatsApp +91 91378 78456 or send the enquiry form with the product, quantity, city and your logo. We usually reply on WhatsApp within 15 minutes.

Step 2

Get a quote and mock-up

We confirm the price and send a PDF mock-up showing exactly where and how your logo will be printed.

Step 3

Approve and pay the advance

Production starts after you approve the mock-up. Customised orders: 50% advance, balance before dispatch. GST invoice with every order.

Step 4

Production at our Bhiwandi factory

Your order is made and printed under one roof at our own factory in Bhiwandi and checked before packing.

Step 5

Delivery or collection

We deliver across India, or you can collect from our Dombivli West shop or Bhiwandi factory outlet (by appointment).

FAQ

Common questions

More answers on our FAQ page.

Do you give a formal quotation for our purchase file?

Yes. We send a quotation on our letterhead with specifications, photos, unit price, GST and delivery terms.

Do you give a GST invoice?

Yes, with every order. Our GSTIN is 27AGCPJ9001C1ZX.

Can you deliver to district offices outside Maharashtra?

Yes. We dispatch to every state by transport or courier. Delivery is charged extra and quoted with the order.

Can our department name or logo be printed?

Yes. Umbrellas, canopy tops and gazebo walls can be printed with a logo, department name or a public message.

What is the minimum order for printed umbrellas?

300 pieces for logo-printed umbrellas. Plain umbrellas, canopy tents and gazebos can be ordered in smaller quantities.
